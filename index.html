What it finds

Duplicates

Pages that should be one

Near-identical pages detected semantically, with a recommended survivor and a merge & redirect action.

Contradictions

Facts that don't agree

Conflicting dates, owners, numbers and processes — with the conflicting claims quoted side by side.

Open questions

Questions nobody answered

Unanswered questions and stale TODOs living inside your pages, surfaced with owners who can close them.

Why it's different

Zero infrastructure

Runs 100% on Atlassian Forge — no vendor servers, no data leaving your Atlassian site except to your own AI provider.

Bring your own AI key

Google Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic — your key, your provider terms, no per-token markup.

Evidence, always

Every finding quotes the exact text it's based on. Ownership is always labelled potential, never asserted.

Humans approve changes

The agent never edits or archives a page on its own. Every action is audited.
